Assumptions

  • Same course as 2025. Each Strava lap from last year is one leg.
  • Watch distance reads 50.6 mi, as it did last year. If it reads closer to 50.2, aim for the official 8:42 average instead.
  • 13:00 of stopped time, spread across the legs. Last year was about 17:00, with roughly 7:30 of that in leg 8.
  • Fitness: Fox Cities Marathon 3:20:06 on Sept 20 (7:35 per watch mile). Recent 15–18 mi long runs around 8:35.
  • The first marathon matches last year. About 3:43 of running through 26.2. The gain comes from holding pace on legs 5–9.

Race rules

  • Heart rate cap through leg 3: ≤145 on flats, low 150s only on climbs. Last year leg 1 averaged 152.
  • Run hills by effort. Legs 1, 3 and 8 carry the most climbing. Give back time there.
  • Bank gently on legs 2 and 4. Slightly quicker, never hard.
  • Hold 8:40–8:50 on legs 5–9. This is where last year slipped to 9:20–9:35.
  • Fuel every 30 min from the start. Carbs on a schedule, not on feel.
  • Walk through aid, don't stand. Getting stops to 10:00 adds 3 minutes of cushion.
