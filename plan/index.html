Paces start from current 1:32 half fitness. Tighten them after the February and May checkpoints. On windy lakefront days or icy footing, run by effort.

Checkpoints: a 4 mi threshold test in week 13 (Feb 2) and the tune-up half in week 25 (May 1). If the half is 1:27-1:28, race at 7:00-7:05. If it's 1:30 or slower, race at 7:15 (3:10).

Mileage builds about 10% a week between cutbacks. Weeks 7, 11 and 15 drop about 15-20%. Weeks 19-21 are the Costa Rica trip (Mar 17-31): no planned running, then a three-week rebuild into the tune-up half. The two 85s (weeks 27-28) are about 20% over your past 70-mile peaks.

Keep hard days hard and easy days easy. Heavy leg lifting goes on the same day as a run workout, at least 6 hours after it, so the next day is real recovery.

The week follows Pfitzinger's pattern: two endurance runs (Sunday long, Wednesday medium-long), one workout (Tuesday), one GA or MP run (Friday), and recovery runs in between. Drills before Tuesday workouts: A-skips, B-skips, high knees, butt kicks and straight-leg bounds, 2x20 m each.

If the long run has to move to Saturday, shift the whole week one day earlier so the workout and the medium-long run keep their spacing.

Lift heavy with crisp reps and stop 1-2 reps short of failure. Soreness that changes your stride the next day means the session was too much.

Guardrails

The last two races both faded late. The main job is arriving in June healthy and durable, not squeezing out extra workouts.

Mileage
The jump to 85 is about 20% over your past peak. If a week leaves you flat, repeat it instead of moving up. If 85 is too much, trim recovery runs first and keep the long run, medium-long run and workout.
Missed days
Don't make them up. Two or more missed in a week means repeating the week, not jumping ahead.
Recovery days
Mon, Thu and Sat stay truly easy (9:00+). Monday can become a rest day or a bike ride if anything aches.
Piriformis watch
If buttock or sciatic tightness comes back, drop RDLs and deadlifts for a week. Keep Lift C, and run the next long run at easy pace only.
Cutback weeks
Weeks 7, 11 and 15 drop about 15-20%. Lifting drops too, but never stops. The Costa Rica trip (weeks 19-21) takes the place of the week 19 and 23 cutbacks.
Costa Rica
Mar 17-31: zero planned running. Any run there is a bonus, and hiking counts. No catch-up miles before or after, and the missed-days rule does not apply. If the first week back (week 22) feels rough, repeat it at ~60 and drop the week 24 VO2max session for an easy 11.
Wisconsin winter
Put Tuesday workouts on the treadmill (1% incline) on icy days. Run by effort into the lake wind, and don't chase splits.
Fueling and sweat
Practice race-day gels and Tailwind on every MP long run and every run of 18+ from week 14 on. Weigh yourself before and after one long run to size up fluid losses.
Sauna
After easy days and lifts is fine. Skip it before quality days, and stop 10 days out from the race.
Shoes
Evo SL for workouts. Run the 18 w/ 12 and 18 w/ 14 @ MP long runs in the Adios Pro 3 to rehearse race day.
Re-check
At week 13 and after the tune-up half, adjust pace zones and the race goal.
